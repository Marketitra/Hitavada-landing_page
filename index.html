A Hitavada Editorial Initiative · Nagpur Bureau Nominations Close: 30 June 2026

FOR over a century, The Hitavada has chronicled the story of Nagpur — its triumphs, its transformations, and the tireless individuals who shape its destiny. Today, we extend that tradition of recognition into a new arena: honouring the businesses and institutions that quietly power this city's momentum.

The Momentum Awards 2026 is a special editorial initiative conceived to shine a well-deserved spotlight on Nagpur's most impactful enterprises — from coaching institutes that have shaped generations of students, to healthcare providers that have redefined care in Vidarbha.

Nominations are now open across eight categories, spanning education, healthcare, retail, real estate, automobile, and hospitality. Slots are deliberately limited to ensure each honouree receives the full weight of The Hitavada's editorial attention.

What Winners Receive
  • Framed Certificate of Excellence
  • 90-second brand feature reel
  • On-premises interview shoot
  • Print editorial spotlight
  • Digital feature on thehitavada.com

"Reaching over two lakh readers across Vidarbha — your story, told by The Hitavada."

Each shortlisted nominee is personally visited by The Hitavada's production team, who craft a 90-second brand feature reel — a cinematic snapshot of the business, its people, and its legacy. The final edit is handed directly to the honouree for use across social media and digital channels.

The editorial feature — both in print and online — reaches an audience of 2 lakh+ readers across the Vidarbha region, offering unparalleled regional visibility for businesses that have earned their place in Nagpur's story.

A Legacy of Trust

Since 1911, The Hitavada has been the newspaper of record for Central India. The Momentum Awards carry forward this legacy of journalistic integrity — every nominee is evaluated by our editorial panel on the basis of community impact, reputation, and reader trust.

Who Should Nominate?

Any business, institution, or professional practice operating in Nagpur is eligible. Whether you are a school that has shaped thousands of futures, a retail brand loved by the city, a healthcare clinic that has served its community for decades, or a real estate firm that has transformed Nagpur's skyline — the Momentum Awards are your platform.

Nominees need not be large corporations. The panel values depth of community impact as much as scale of operation. Family-run enterprises, single-location clinics, and neighbourhood coaching centres are as welcome as regional chains.

"Limited slots per category — submit early to secure your place."

Nominations take under two minutes to submit. Our team reviews every entry within 48 hours and personally contacts each nominee to walk them through the programme details.

The felicitation ceremony will be held at a venue to be announced in Nagpur, with coverage published in The Hitavada print edition and on thehitavada.com.
